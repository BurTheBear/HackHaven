# ECHO

**What if the only way to see was to make noise?**

## Description
You fall into a lightless cave with no lantern. Call out and the cave shows itself in a pulse of sound, but the Listeners hear it too. Ping, listen, throw a pebble to lure them away, and find your way back to the surface. A short (about 8 minute), replayable first-person echolocation horror game with ranks, secrets and challenge modes.

## Controls
- WASD / left stick: move. Mouse / right stick: look
- Left click / Q: call out (ping). Whisper, chalk and pebbles are on the HUD
- Shift: run (loud). Ctrl / C: sneak (silent)
- Right click (hold): aim and throw a pebble
- E: interact, hide, play recordings
- Hold breath: see the in-game prompt
- M: map. Esc: pause

## Features
- Echolocation: the world only exists where your sound has reached
- Listeners hunt by sound, never by sight
- Pebbles, whispers and chalk marks as tools; hold your breath and hide
- Mara's voice logs and hidden journal pages (secrets count and a 100% badge)
- Ranks: GHOST, SILENT, ECHO, SURVIVOR, LOUD
- Challenge modes after your first escape: No pings, One pebble, Total darkness
- Two levels, color-reveal ending with a twist
- Accessibility: subtitles, sound captions, reduced shake, no-flash mode
